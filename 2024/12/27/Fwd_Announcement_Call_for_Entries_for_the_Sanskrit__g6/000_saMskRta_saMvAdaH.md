The **Kaling Literary Festival** 2024 is announcing an Award for Excellence in Sanskrit Literature to recognize outstanding contributions to the promotion and preservation of Sanskrit, with eligibility for books published between January and December 2024. The award includes a cash prize of ₹1,00,000, a memento, and a certificate of recognition. Nominations can be made by authors, publishers, or institutions, requiring five copies of the book, the author's biography and contact details, and a brief synopsis in English, Sanskrit, Odia, or Hindi, to be submitted by January 15, 2025. Evaluation will focus on literary quality, originality, contribution to Sanskrit scholarship, and relevance to contemporary or traditional themes. The award will be presented at the KLF Book Awards on February 15, 2025 in New Delhi.
