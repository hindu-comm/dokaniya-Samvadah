In celebration of **World Sanskrit Day**, **AuroBharati, Sri Aurobindo Society** presents a **week-long celebration** on **‘Vedic Heritage and Tradition’** from **19-25 August 2024**.  
  
**Event Schedule**:  
  
- **19.08.2024 (Monday) 10:00 am**  
 **Celebration of World Sanskrit Day** & **Symposium**: \*‘Vedic Heritage in the Light of Sri Aurobindo: Intangible Culture, Tradition & Continuity’\* at Sri Aurobindo Society, Puducherry  
  
- **20.08.2024 (Tuesday) 10:00 am**  
 **Workshop**: \*‘The Power of Chanting the Vedic Mantras: Therapeutic Use’\* at Matriniketan, Sri Aurobindo Society, Puducherry  
  
- **21.08.2024 (Wednesday) 04:00 pm**  
 **Visit** to French Institute of Pondicherry (IFP) & interaction with faculty on **Vedic Manuscript Collection**  
  
- **22.08.2024 (Thursday) 05:00 pm**  
 **Screening** of Sri Aurobindo's **‘Hymn to Durga’** (Durga Stotram) in Sanskrit on AuroBharati's Facebook page  
  
- **23.08.2024 (Friday) 05:00 pm**  
 **Screening** of film **‘Sri Aurobindo - Glimpses of His Life’** in Sanskrit  
  
- **24.08.2024 (Saturday) 09:00 am**  
 **Workshop**: \*‘Balasamskritam: Sanskrit for Children’\* at Sri Aurobindo Society, Puducherry  
  
- **25.08.2024 (Sunday) 09:00 am**  
 Selected **chantings of the Vedas** on **Devavani-AuroBharati Sanskrit Radio**
