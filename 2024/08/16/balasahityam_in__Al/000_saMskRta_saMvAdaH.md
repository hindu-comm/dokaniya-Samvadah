[[संस्कृत संवादः	2024-08-16, 13:52:24 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/Samvadah/c/AlfbWzk7i5k)]]

Sri Aurobindo Society, Puducherry, has launched a new web portal, **‘Bal Sahitya-Towards Creativity & Self-Expression,’** to promote **children's literature**. The portal, accessible at [balasahityam.in](http://balasahityam.in) aims to inspire creativity and emotional understanding in young readers through **stories, poems**, and **educational content**.  
  
Launched on Independence Day and **Sri Aurobindo's birth anniversary**, the portal was inaugurated by **Shri Pradeep Narang**, Chairman of the Society, and features contributions from prominent figures like **Dr. Rutger Kortenhorst** and **Shri V. Kaliaperumal**. The initiative includes a **panel discussion** on children’s literature's role in fostering self-expression.
