Course Overview:

Samskrita Karyashala offers a unique course combining the Paninian approach with modern pedagogy for accelerated and conceptual learning of Samskrita. This course uses advanced technological tools for effective learning.

  

Course Structure:

\- Interactive and Intensive Workshop

\- Adequate Practice during the Workshop

\- Mentoring and Discussion Groups

\- Application in Ramayana

  

Course Outcomes:

\- Strong foundation in Samskrita language and grammar.

\- Understanding of Paninian Sutras.

\- Ability to decipher Samskrita works and use commentaries.

\- Preparation for advanced Samskrita studies.

  

Materials Provided:

\- Session Recordings (Online Batch only)

\- Presentations

\- Mind-Maps

\- Quizzes

  

  

Prerequisites:

\- Passion for learning Samskrita with a long-term vision to read Indic texts.

\- Commitment to attend all sessions and practice for at least 2 hours weekly.

\- Ability to read Devanagari script.

\- Analytical thinking.

\- Proficiency in English.

\- Basic computer literacy.
