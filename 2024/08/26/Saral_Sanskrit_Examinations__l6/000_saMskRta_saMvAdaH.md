**About the Saral Sanskrit Examinations**  
Since 1956, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan has been conducting Saral Sanskrit Examinations twice a year (February and September) to promote Sanskrit among the younger generation. These examinations are available for five graded courses:  
- **Balabodha**  
- **Prarambha**  
- **Pravesha**  
- **Parichaya**  
- **Kovida**  
Conducted at over **200 examination centers** in India and abroad, these examinations have benefited **21,85,538 students** as of 2021.  
  
**Examination Structure**  
- **Sanskrit Balabodha:** One Question Paper  
- **Sanskrit Prarambha:** One Question Paper  
- **Sanskrit Pravesha:** Two Question Papers  
- **Sanskrit Parichaya:** Three Question Papers  
- **Sanskrit Kovida:** Four Question Papers
