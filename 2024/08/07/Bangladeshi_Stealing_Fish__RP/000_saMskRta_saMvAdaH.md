*Suhas Mahesh*remembered this verse seeing the stealing of fish from prime minister's residence in Bangladesh amid coup.  
  
श्रीकथारत्नाकरे श्रीहेमविजयरचिते।  
॥४॥तत्कालमतौ दण्डिकथा  
  
धूर्त्तानां चरितं वक्तुमशक्यं यतस्तैर्जगदपि वञ्च्यते। यतः  

> कक्षे किम्। ननु पुस्तकम्।  
> गलति किम्। काव्यस्य सारो रसः।  
> पुच्छं किम्। ननु ताडपत्ररचना।  
> रेखाः कथम्। पङ्क्तयः॥  
> गन्धः किम्। ननु युद्धबद्धरचना रामायणी भारती।

  
इत्येवं ननु गौडदेशपुरुषैर्धूर्तैर्जगद्वञ्च्यते॥१॥  
  
Rough Translation —  
  
**An Ancient Sanskrit Verse on a Bengali Caught carrying a Fish :**  
  
**Questioner:** "What's under your arm?"  
**Gauda Man:** "A book."  
  
**Questioner:** "what's dripping?"  
**Gauda Man:** "It's rasa in the story."  
  
**Questioner:** "I see a tail."  
**Gauda Man:** "Palm leaves."  
  
**Questioner:** "I can see scales."  
**Gauda Man:** "Lines."  
  
**Questioner:** "Why does it smell?"  
**Gauda Man:** "Stench from the battle scenes of the Indian Epic of Ramayana."  
  
Thus, the inhabitants of Gauda (the central part of Bengal, stretching from Banga to Bhubaneshwar in Orrissa) cleverly deceive the world.
