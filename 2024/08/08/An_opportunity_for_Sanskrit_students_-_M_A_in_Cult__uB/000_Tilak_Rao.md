**M.A in Cultural Studies**

Sanskrit students who wish to explore new academic paths after their degree program can apply for the MA in Cultural Studies Program. This course addresses questions related to the contemporary relevance of Shāstras, translation, critical edition, culture, religion, language,
and more. To prepare students in this field, the program integrates diverse methods, theories, and best practices, catering to those from a wide range of disciplines, including Science and Technology, Social Sciences, Humanities, and Performing Arts. This course immerses students in an academic environment dedicated to the rigorous scientific study of cultural differences, with a particular focus on Indian culture.

  

Upon completion of this program, students will be equipped to conduct research across various domains, including Hinduism Studies, Indian Knowledge Systems, Cultural Studies, Sanskrit Studies, and other related fields.
