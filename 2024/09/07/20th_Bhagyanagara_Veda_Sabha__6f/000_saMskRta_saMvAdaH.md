**20th Bhagyanagara Veda Sabha**  
  
**The Nori Narasimha Sastry Charitable Trust** invites all Veda believers to the 20th Bhagyanagara Veda Sabha, taking place at Sri Kalyanananda Bharathi Kshetram, Parvatapur, Uppal, Hyderabad on September 15th, 2024, from 7:30 am to 7:30 pm. Approximately 40 Ghanapathins and Kramapathins from Telangana and AP are expected to participate.  
  
**Event Details**  
  
\* Date: September 15th, 2024 (Sunday)  
\* Time: 7:30 am - 7:30 pm  
\* Venue: Sri Kalyanananda Bharathi Kshetram, Parvatapur, Uppal, Hyderabad  
\* Format: Pareekshaa paddhati, with 12 rounds of recitation by assembled scholars  
\* Results of the test for new Vedic scholars will be announced on the day  
\* Lunch will be served at the venue  
  
**Frequently Asked Questions**  
\* **What is a Veda Sabha?**: An assembly of Vedic scholars, each knowing at least one Veda completely.  
\* **Why is it conducted?**: For lokakalyāṇam (the general good of all) and to display the knowledge of scholars.  
\* **What is the format?**: The parīkṣa method, where scholars recite from random points in each kāṇḍa.  
\* **Why follow the Parīkṣa format?**: To demonstrate the mastery of scholars, not to test them.  
\* **Food and drink**: Traditional lunch will be served between 1 pm and 3 pm.  
\* **Inviting others**: Yes, please invite friends and family.  
\* **Contributions**: Not required, but a Hundi will be kept for voluntary contributions.  
\* **Recording**: Generally not allowed, but exceptions can be made with permission from the organisers.  
\* **Dress code**: No specific dress code, but attendees are requested to be in a clean state.  
\* **Duration**: Attendees can stay for as long as they like, but 45 minutes in either half will allow them to hear all four Vedas.  
\* **Venue location**: Sri Kalyananada Bharathi Kshetram, about 8.5 km from Uppal Metro Station.  
\* **Parking**: No separate facility, but vehicles can be parked on nearby roads without causing obstruction.
