Whether you're studying complex texts or communicating in Sanskrit, these two tools will provide the support you need all in your smartphone.  
  
☞ ***Google Translate: Tap to Translate***  
  
**» Youtube guide**  
[https://youtube.com/shorts/vCoFb62Ug1Q](https://youtube.com/shorts/vCoFb62Ug1Q)  
  
**Step 1: Install Google Translate**  
  
**Android (Play Store)** [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.translate](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.translate)  
  
**Apple iphone (App store)**  
[https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-translate/id414706506](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-translate/id414706506)  
  
**Step 2: Enable Tap to Translate**  
- Open the Google Translate app.  
- Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner.  
- Select "Settings".  
- Tap "Tap to Translate".  
- Toggle on "Enable Tap to Translate".  
  
**Step 3: Using Tap to Translate**  
- Copy any text you want to translate.  
- Tap the Google Translate bubble that appears on the screen.  
- The translation will be displayed in the Google Translate window.  
  
☞ ***Gboard Translate Feature***  
  
**» Youtube guide**  
[https://youtube.com/shorts/pJ99dlraqEY](https://youtube.com/shorts/pJ99dlraqEY)  
  
**Step 1: Install Gboard**  
**Android (Play Store)**  
[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.inputmethod.latin](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.inputmethod.latin)  
  
**Apple iphone (App store)**  
[https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gboard-the-google-keyboard/id1091700242](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gboard-the-google-keyboard/id1091700242)  
  
**Step 2: Access Gboard Translate**  
- Open any app where you can type, like Messages or WhatsApp.  
- Tap on a text field to bring up the keyboard.  
- Tap on the "Google Translate" icon (a G文 icon usually found in the toolbar above the keyboard) or tap settings and select the G文 icon.  
  
**Step 3: Translate Text Using Gboard**  
- Type the text you want to translate in the text field.  
- Select the source language and the target language by tapping on the language names.  
- Gboard will automatically translate the text as you type and show the translation in the text field.
