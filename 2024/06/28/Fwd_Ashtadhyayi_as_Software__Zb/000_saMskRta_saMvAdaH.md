Namassarvebhyah,

I have two related announcements.

1\. **Ashtadhyayi now available as executable Software**  

I'd like to announce the availability of

  

**PAIAS**, a Python-based machine interpreter of the entire Ashtadhyayi text corpus (sutras and paathas)

[https://gitlab.com/vedavaapi/apps/ashtadhyayi/](https://gitlab.com/vedavaapi/apps/ashtadhyayi/)

  

And a live web application using it for Krdanta ruupa siddhi to prove that it works.

[https://paias.sambhasha.ksu.ac.in/](https://paias.sambhasha.ksu.ac.in/)

  

[![](https://ci3.googleusercontent.com/proxy/89sCg6DpdoYm-oQuAUOrgUiEnzm9ugqbmezlAxsbDFSieC2pnXvIJJq0HJb7c2CBuksrDzcg2teDRXaG_nuaU9fllgESeCxSkl_gIGBdxTJ1X6945w=s0-d-e1-ft#https://ssl.gstatic.com/docs/doclist/images/icon_11_image_list.png)

This is the output of Sarada Susarla's PhD work under the guidance of Prof. Shivani at Karnataka Sanskrit University and Prof. Tilak Rao (Chanakya University).

  

PAIAS is available as both an open-source python library and Flask Web service. You can do things like

\- Ashtadhyayi().interpret(\[gamlr, Satr\]) -\> gacChat

\- Ashtadhyayi().compute_samjna("upadhA", "paTh") -\> "a"

  

This lets a learner play with ashtadhyayi engine and explore what-if scenarios, find what sutras apply in a given context etc. This dramatically simplifies and shortens the time and effort to understand Ashtadhyayi, especially by non-traditional learners without memorization.

This project started as a casual hallway conversation at MIT School of Vedic Sciences Pune in 2018. Prof.Tilak Rao's tremendous grasp of Ashtadhyayi prakriya and insights helped shape this solution.

  

2\. Based on the above experience, we are launching a new 3-credit online course called **Ashtadhyayi as Software** for science and engineering people, starting July 24th. Students will get first hand experience in using and enhancing the software to unleash the full power of Ashtadhyayi, and hopefully apply its techniques for other Indian languages as well.

  

Here is a doc describing the course:

[https://docs.google.com/document/d/197QUhfFNArdtteBlgihV42HN_5OLsaG9e7BayEZKCpY/edit?usp=drivesdk](https://docs.google.com/document/d/197QUhfFNArdtteBlgihV42HN_5OLsaG9e7BayEZKCpY/edit?usp=drivesdk)

It has links to research papers describing our work.

  

The course is now open for enrollment at

[https://siddhantavijnan.org/product/ashtadhyayi-as-software/](https://siddhantavijnan.org/product/ashtadhyayi-as-software/)

  

Ashtadhyayi interpretation has been a well-trodden path for decades. Many scholars on this list have done seminal work in this area. Ours is a humble attempt to build on their insights to make this monumental treatise more intelligible to the young generation.
