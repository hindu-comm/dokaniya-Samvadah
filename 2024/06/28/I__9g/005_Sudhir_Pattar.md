I respectfully disagree with your opinion. To me, Samskrit Bharati is a great starting point. There maybe some who can directly jump into 10 lakaras and start using लिट् लुङ् लृङ् lakars in daily conversation, but starting with the क्त्वतुँ and क्त्वा and others as is a great way to get into the world of Samskrit for many like me.

  

I started my journey with Samskrit Bharati and today am able to read and understand bhashyas of great acharyas, kavyas and still I cannot say that I know all the 4000 Panini sutras. I am eternally grateful to Samskrit Bharati. Without them, I couldn’t have entered into this.

  

Samskrit is an ocean and one has to start somewhere. I support Samskrit Bharati and applaud their efforts. I have seen vererans make grammatical mistakes, and it’s okay because one can learn from mistakes.  

  

Regards,
