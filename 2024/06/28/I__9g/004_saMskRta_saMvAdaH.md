People target me for criticizing Samskrita Bharati.  
  
While the entire purpose of Sanskrit was to eliminate impurities from human speech and make it pure. Instead, you at Samskrita Bharati have deliberately allowed those very impurities to creep in — all in the name of making Sanskrit "more accessible" to the masses — and you dare call this preservation?  
  
To me, it's like deliberately splashing ink on the pristine white garments of Vāgdevī so she appears more "human" and people find her easier to connect to.  
  
This is not personal enmity toward your institution. I value even the smallest pebble of genuine welfare for Sanskrit as much as a boulder.  
  
But it is high time you realize that what you have done is far more malicious than noble.  
  
I do not believe you will ever abandon your inherent nature, but I have always opposed this ideology and I will continue to do so.
