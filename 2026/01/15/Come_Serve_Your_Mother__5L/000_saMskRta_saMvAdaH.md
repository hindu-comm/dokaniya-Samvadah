As you can see, our posts have become fewer and irregular—owing to personal issues about which I never wished to lament or complain. After brooding over whether to write this or not for nearly three months, I am finally writing it today on this special occasion, to awaken whatever sense of Dharma remains.  
  
It is precisely to revive and sustain this dharma that we urgently need many committed volunteers to carry forward the work of **संस्कृत संवादः** — for serving Sanskrit to its fullest potential.  
  
This endeavor — the work of **संस्कृत संवादः** — should ideally be led by Brahmins, which, despite my efforts, has not yet been achieved. Why Brahmins? Why does birth matter? Bluntly: because you cannot escape what has been inherited by you—the physical body, mental tendencies, relations, family, wealth or varna. Opposing this natural birth-based order seems hypocritical when you are claiming all other inheritance.  
  
This is the dharma of serving the Almighty through serving Vāgdevi, the embodiment of the perfection of speech. I believe that the benefits received by anyone—regardless of caste or gender—are the same if the work done (in absolute terms) is the same. However, even the same benefit can be relatively greater for a Brahmin than for a non-Brahmin. Purity (of any kind) matters far more to someone who is actively pursuing it than to someone with a history of neglecting it.  
  
Thus, a non-Brahmin can also attain liberation through this dharma. But it may take many births for a non-Brahmin, whereas it may happen in a single birth for a Brahmin.  
  
Without delving into the complexities of the science of varṇāśrama dharma, let us focus on the real karma.  
  
Simply put: preserve Sanskrit, no matter who you are and if you are a Brahmin, strive to lead by example.  
  
Volunteers must be ready to accomplish any assigned task with sincere devotion. Discipline, commitment, and obedience would be given the highest priority here.  
  
Only merit will earn you a higher position in **संस्कृत संवादः** — not birth-based claims—because this organization is not inherited; it has been formed.  
  
So, ignite the fire of Sanskrit in your own heart—and then kindle it in the hearts of others—by joining our endeavor.
