**The Vaidika Bharata Gurukula-Goshala**, founded in 2019 by **Acharya Mohit Bharadvaja**, is committed to reviving Vaidika traditions in the Sarasvati, Yamuna, and Ganga regions. With a decade of dedication, Acharya Mohit has invested his savings to sustain this mission.  
  
Currently, the Gurukula houses 12 Brahmacharis studying Yajurveda and Atharvaveda, and is developing a second Goshala on 5+ acres, operational with 14 Brahmacharis and over 35 cows. The students receive a holistic education that combines traditional and modern subjects.  
  
To continue their efforts, they urgently need funds. Your support will help preserve the critically endangered Rgveda Shankhayana Shakha and protect India’s ancient heritage.
