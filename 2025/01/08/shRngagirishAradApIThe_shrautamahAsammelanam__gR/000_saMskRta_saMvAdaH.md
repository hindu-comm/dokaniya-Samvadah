[[संस्कृत संवादः	2025-01-08, 00:13:42 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/Samvadah/c/gR4JfdLBS0k)]]

With the blessings of the Ubhaya Jagadgurus, the Annual Shrauta Maha Sammelanam is scheduled to take place at Narasimhavanam, Sringeri, from January 6 to 8, 2025.  

  

This three-day event will include:

  

\- Parayana of the Veda, Vedanga, and Kalpasutra

\- Vidvat Ghoshthi on Veda Bhashya in the esteemed presence of the Jagadguru
